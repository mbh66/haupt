# I help the people who look after land get paid for the water it holds.

## Greyton, Valley of Grace

I want more value to flow into the living parts of a place than the extractive parts can take out of it. The water held in the land is where I start, and I publish how it's done.

Each letter in [[letters/index|the Letter Book]] went privately to one person who can move a field. It's published once they reply, or once they've had time to. Results are shown either way.
