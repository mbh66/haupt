# I do for water held in the land what De Beers did for diamonds, except I publish the playbook.

## Greyton, Valley of Grace

Each letter in [[letters/index|the Letter Book]] went privately to one person who can move a field. It is published once they reply, or once they have had time to. Results are shown either way.
