Some people work on a satellite network and decide to go to Mars. I worked on one and moved to Greyton. From here, in the Valley of Grace in South Africa's Western Cape, I help catchments and their landowners make the water held in living land worth paying for, and I publish how it is done.

## The view from orbit

I was part of the launch of Iridium, the satellite phone network that covers the whole planet, poles and oceans included [MS]. My work was systems integration for its gateways, the ground stations where the network meets the telephone systems of a particular place: the South Pacific gateway in Melbourne, the head office and North American gateway in Reston, Virginia, and the South American gateway in Mexico City [MS].

The satellites passed calls between them in orbit, and the system worked. Too few people believed they needed it, and the company went bankrupt within a year of launch; new owners and a defence contract rescued it [IC]. That taught me the first lesson of this work: supply without belief goes nowhere.

I left before the collapse. Alchemists develop a nose for these things.

## As good as paper

From January 2007 to July 2010 I was Managing Director of Striata UK, building the business across the UK and continental Europe in secure document delivery for regulated industries [MS]. At Striata we worked with Barclays, HSBC, Hutchison 3G, Western Union, O2, BT, Vodafone and Bank of America [MS].

The real work was persuading banks, telecoms companies, regulators and the people receiving the documents to accept an emailed statement as good as a paper one. Even recipients found it hard at first. That was my own Bretton Woods, and the second lesson: belief holds once an institution stands behind it.

## Forty years of apprenticeship

Those were two chapters in more than forty years of change management, telecommunications and systems integration, across several countries [MS]. I have also advised leaders and families privately; their names stay private.

This site marks my sixtieth birthday, and I treat it as the halfway mark. Everything before was training wheels and practice runs. This site starts the second half, and it begins with letters.

## The valley

The valley is where the lessons were tested, and it remains the proof site. In September 2026 I convened the first meeting of the [Ward 2 Business and Community Development Forum](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za), with Stalin Govender of Theewaterskloof Municipality [IC]. We got parties into the room who had not spoken to each other before, and the Forum agreed five priorities and meets every two months [IC]. I have hosted four international visitors who came to see the work [MS].

The Forum's register keeps the record of that work, including [my part in it](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/contributors/michael-haupt). What the valley teaches travels: the [[method|method]] is written for any catchment.
